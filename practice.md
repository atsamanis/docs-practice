# How to make authentic carbonara

## Ingredients

- Linguine
- 3 eggs
- 300gr Parmiggiano Regiano
- Salt
- Pepper
- 200gr Guanciale

## Steps

1. Break the eggs and pour into a bowl

2. Pour 100 grams of grated Parmiggiano in the bowl

3. Add pepper to the bowl and mix them all together until a creamy paste is formed.

4. Boil the linguine into Al dente according to the time mentioned on the package.

5. Dice the guanciale and add it to another pan in medium heat until golden/brown.

6. When the linguine is ready, empty them in the pan with the guanciale and mix. Add the egg/cheese mix while stirring slowly.

7. Finally, serve them with a little more Parmiggiano and a little more pepper.

8. Enjoy!

You can also watch it on [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dsg5H2TEQXs)

> Be careful so that the egg/cheese mixture does not turn into omelette while stirring with the Linguine!
